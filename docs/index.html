Policy papers for Brrk.

Brrk turns paper books and PDFs into readable Markdown, notes, and vocabulary on Android. These public pages explain how the app handles data and the terms for using the beta.

Record 01

Local by default

Imported PDFs, captured images, OCR Markdown, manual edits, notes, tags, vocabulary, and cache data are stored in app-private storage on the device.

Record 02

User-started network use

Brrk sends data to Mistral only when you start OCR or vocabulary lookup. The app does not include ads, analytics, accounts, or telemetry.

Google Play note: Use the Privacy Policy URL in Play Console. The policy is an active public HTML page, not a PDF, and includes a contact mechanism for privacy inquiries.
